What is Investors in People?

Investors in People (IIP) is an internationally recognised standard that sets a benchmark for excellent people management. It helps organisations of all sizes understand how effectively they lead, manage and develop their people — and provides a clear framework for ongoing improvement.

Accreditation demonstrates to employees, customers and stakeholders that your organisation is genuinely committed to its people. There are four levels of achievement — Standard, Silver, Gold and Platinum — each reflecting a deeper maturity in how you invest in your workforce.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Investors in People (IIP)?

Investors in People is an internationally recognised accreditation framework that helps organisations improve performance through better people management. It sets a standard for how organisations lead, support and develop their people.

How long does it take to achieve IIP accreditation?

The timeline varies depending on your organisation's size and current maturity. Typically, organisations prepare over 3–12 months, with our support helping you identify gaps and build readiness at the right pace.

Do I need a consultant to achieve IIP accreditation?

While not mandatory, an independent IIP consultant provides an objective perspective, expert knowledge of the framework, and practical support that significantly improves your chances of achieving — and maintaining — accreditation.

What is the difference between IIP Standard, Silver, Gold and Platinum?

Investors in People has four levels of accreditation: Standard, Silver, Gold and Platinum. Each level reflects a greater maturity in how the organisation manages, develops and supports its people. We help organisations achieve whichever level is right for their ambition.
