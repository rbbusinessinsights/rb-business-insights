Richard Budd

Founder & Principal Consultant

Richard is a highly experienced organisational development consultant specialising in Investors in People, employee engagement, leadership development and business improvement. With more than 17 years' experience supporting organisations across a wide range of sectors, Richard helps clients turn insight into practical action and sustainable improvement.

Before launching RB Business Insights, Richard held senior leadership and business development roles with organisations including Grant Thornton, Exemplas, Sainsbury's, Hollywood Bowl, and Tenpin. His background combines strategic consultancy with hands-on operational leadership, giving him a practical understanding of how organisations can improve performance through their people.

  • Investors in People assessments and support
  • Organisational development and change management
  • Leadership development and coaching
  • Employee engagement and culture work

Gary Inman

Organisational Development Specialist & Partner
Independent Contractor

Gary is passionate about creating positive workplace cultures based on respect, inclusion, collaboration and empowerment. For more than 20 years he's been helping organisations to get the best from their people, challenging and extending practices, widening horizons and helping to create brilliant places to work.

Not just because that makes for happier, more fulfilling lives for everyone (though that's reason enough), but because that's also the way to deliver the best outcomes for the business and for customers. His wide experience as an Investors in People specialist has exposed him to hundreds of forward-thinking organisations and given him a huge base of experience and insight to draw upon.

Gary is striving to make the world a better place: taking what we know and understand about people, and inspiring others to new and exciting visions, to build workplaces that support, grow, excite and inspire, that are safe but stimulating, and that deliver sustainable high performance.

  • Investors in People specialist
