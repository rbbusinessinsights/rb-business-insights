Leadership Competencies Explorer

A practical guide to the 38 Korn Ferry Leadership Architect® competencies. Use it to build management capability, find where your gaps may be, and see how your own view of your leadership compares with the people who work closest to you.

By Richard Budd · Resource · 15 min to explore

How to use this resource

Three ways to put the framework to work, whether you are developing yourself, your managers or your leadership team.

🧭

Develop management competencies

Move beyond a vague idea of “good management” and give people a shared language for what effective leadership looks like in practice.

  • Set clear expectations for managers at each level
  • Shape development plans and coaching conversations
  • Inform leadership programmes and succession planning
🔍

Identify where gaps may exist

Review each competency honestly and mark it as a strength, a development area or a likely gap. Patterns quickly show where to focus.

  • Spot gaps for an individual, a team or a whole management group
  • Separate what matters most from what is simply nice to have
  • Avoid trying to fix all 38 at once
🪞

Peer-to-peer leadership reflection

Ask colleagues who work closely with you to rate you using the same competencies, then compare how they see your strengths with your own perception.

  • Discover strengths you under-estimate
  • Uncover blind spots you may not see
  • Turn comparison into an open, honest conversation

A simple way to work through it

ExploreOpen each category and read the key behaviours that sit behind each competency.
ReflectRate yourself: strength, developing or gap. Go with your first instinct.
Invite peersAsk 3–5 trusted colleagues to do the same about you, on their own copy.
CompareEnter their view and see where perceptions match, and where they differ.
ActChoose two or three priorities and agree practical next steps.
Tip for peer reflection: agree the ground rules first. Feedback should focus on observable behaviours, be given to help rather than judge, and be received with curiosity. The biggest insights often come from the differences, not the agreement.

About the framework

The Korn Ferry Leadership Architect® framework is one of the most widely recognised leadership competency models used globally. Organised into four factors and twelve clusters (A–L), it helps organisations understand the behaviours that contribute to individual, team and organisational success.

RB Business Insights uses competency frameworks such as this to support leadership development, talent strategies, succession planning, performance discussions and organisational capability reviews. While organisations may choose to assess all 38 competencies, the greatest impact often comes from identifying the key leadership behaviours that will make the biggest difference to performance, culture and long-term success.

The summaries on this page are an independent overview and interpretation of the 38 Korn Ferry Leadership Architect® competencies. Korn Ferry owns all intellectual property relating to the framework. This page is intended as an educational resource and is not a reproduction of official Korn Ferry material.
