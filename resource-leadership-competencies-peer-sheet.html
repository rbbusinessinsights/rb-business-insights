Leadership Competencies: Peer Feedback Sheet

Based on the Korn Ferry Leadership Architect® competencies · RB Business Insights

How to complete this sheet
  1. Think about the person named above and what you have observed them doing in the last 6–12 months.
  2. For each of the 38 competencies, tick one box: Strength, Developing or Gap.
  3. To make you prioritise, choose exactly 12 Strengths, 14 Developing and 12 Gaps. Use the tally below to keep count.
  4. Add a few comments on the last page, then give the sheet to the person it is about.

Strength: a consistent, visible strength of theirs. Developing: sometimes shown; room to grow. Gap: where you most often see them struggle or where development would help most. Be honest and constructive: this is to help, not to judge.
