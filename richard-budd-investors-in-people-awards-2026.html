Richard Budd Celebrates Investors in People Awards 2026 Win

By RB Business InsightsNovember 23, 2026

Recognising a commitment to better workplaces

Richard Budd, Founder and Principal Consultant at RB Business Insights, is an Investors in People Awards 2026 winner in the Central region.

We're delighted to share this recognition of Richard's work as an Investors in People consultant and his commitment to helping organisations create workplaces where people and performance can thrive together.

Through RB Business Insights, Richard works alongside leaders and teams on employee engagement, leadership development and organisational improvement. His approach focuses on listening to people and turning insight into practical action.

Thank you to the organisations and people who have shared their experiences and been part of this journey. We look forward to continuing to support organisations to build stronger cultures and better workplaces.
